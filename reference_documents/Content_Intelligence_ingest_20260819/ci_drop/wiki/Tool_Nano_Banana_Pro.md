Image model used for **face consistency**, character sheets and wardrobe
elements in the Seedance workflows above.

## Role in the stack

The face-and-costume half of a two-model image split: Nano Banana Pro for
character sheets and outfits, [[Tool_Seedream_5_0_Pro]] for environments.

tagged elements) carried into the video prompt.
